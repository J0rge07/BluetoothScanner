Bluetooth Scanner documentation
===============================

A tkinter GUI that scans for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices with ``bleak``,
refreshes every 2 seconds sorted from strongest to weakest signal, and lets the
user connect to a device, browse its services and characteristics, and read,
write, or subscribe to notifications.
